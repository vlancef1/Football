Route Tree Quiz

Routes from Every Receiver Route in American Football explained in 5 minutes. Name the route from the diagram, or pick the diagram for a route name.

Diagrams: the circle is the player (WR = outside receiver, Slot = slot receiver or tight end, RB = running back). The thick line on the left is the sideline, the dashed line is the line of scrimmage, the tick marks are the hash, and the middle of the field is to the right.
